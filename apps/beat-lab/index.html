About

Beat Lab is a 16-step drum machine for sketching ideas in seconds. Open it, tap in a pattern, tweak the swing, and export a WAV to drop into your DAW.

It ships with a dozen kits and loads your own samples from any folder.

Features

  • 16-step sequencer with up to 8 tracks and 16 patterns
  • Swing, per-step velocity and probability
  • 12 built-in kits plus drag-and-drop user samples
  • WAV and MIDI export
  • Low-latency ASIO and WASAPI output
