About

TheosWorld Radio streams internet radio from around the world without the bloat. Pick a station, hit play, and let it run in the system tray while you work.

Browse by genre, country or language, save unlimited favourites, and record a station to MP3 with one click. The player uses almost no CPU and remembers where you left off.

Features

  • Over 30,000 stations, searchable by name, genre and country
  • Favourites, recently played, and a sleep timer
  • Record streams to MP3 with automatic track splitting
  • Global media keys and a compact tray mode
  • Portable mode: run it from a USB stick
