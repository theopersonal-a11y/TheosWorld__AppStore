About

Lumos is an AI coding assistant for your desktop. Point it at a project folder and ask it to explain code, fix bugs, add features or run your builds and tests.

Lumos is 100% free. There are no subscriptions, no paid tiers and no usage limits to buy your way out of. It needs an internet connection to work and about 1 GB of free storage.
