About

Dungeon Dash is a bite-sized roguelike where the floor collapses behind you. Dash through procedurally generated rooms, grab relics that stack in wild ways, and see how deep you can get in five minutes.

Runs are short, deaths are fast, and there is always one more relic to unlock.

Features

  • Procedural dungeons with 6 biomes and 40+ enemy types
  • 120 relics with synergies to discover
  • Daily seeded challenge with a local leaderboard
  • Full gamepad support and remappable keys
  • Runs at 60 fps on integrated graphics
