About

Sky Watch is a small always-on-top weather widget you can park in a corner of your desktop. It shows the current conditions, an hourly strip, and a 7-day forecast without opening a browser.

It polls a free weather API every 15 minutes and otherwise does nothing in the background.

Features

  • Hourly and 7-day forecast with rain probability
  • Rain and severe weather alerts as toast notifications
  • Sunrise / sunset ring and moon phase
  • Multiple locations with quick switching
  • Light, dark and transparent themes
